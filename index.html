01 / ABOUT ME

I like building the whole system, not just the screen.

I’m Saimon Kabir Chowdhury — a product-minded developer who enjoys turning rough ideas into working software. I’m most comfortable when a project needs both strong interface thinking and real technical depth.

I started from mobile development and kept moving deeper into the stack: native Android, Flutter, APIs, realtime systems, databases and backend services. That naturally pulled me toward AI and computer vision, because the most interesting products often need more than a polished UI — they need software that can understand, predict or automate.

I approach projects as systems. What does the user need to understand? What data moves behind the screen? Where can the flow fail? What should happen next? I enjoy mapping those questions, designing the experience, and then figuring out how to make the product actually work.
