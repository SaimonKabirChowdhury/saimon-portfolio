Developer by craft.
Builder by instinct.

I’m Saimon Kabir Chowdhury. I’ve spent years moving between mobile development, product interfaces, backend systems, AI experiments and visual tools. The common thread is simple: I like understanding the whole machine, not only the screen I’m assigned.

I’m strongest when a project needs someone who can think about the user flow, the data moving underneath it, the business logic and the way the final thing should feel. I’m especially drawn to mobility, AI, computer vision, multi-role applications and tools that help people make faster decisions.
