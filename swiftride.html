CASE STUDY / SWIFTRIDE

Making the pickup
less uncertain.

SwiftRide is a mobile UX concept for the most fragile part of a ride: finding the right driver, finding the right rider, and knowing what happens next.

Scope

Rider, driver and dispatch experiences designed in Penpot.

Focus

Search, matching, pickup verification, safety and route context.

Status

Self-initiated product design case study.

THE PRODUCT QUESTION

How do you make a street-side handoff feel clear for both people?

Riders need to know where the car is and whether it is theirs. Drivers need an unambiguous pickup location, a fast way to verify the rider, and a route that does not disappear behind product chrome. The design treats these as one shared moment—not three disconnected screens.

01

Rider

Find a destination, compare the ride, see the driver approach and use safety controls while travelling.

02

Driver

Accept a job, navigate with pickup context, confirm with an OTP and record the work.

03

Operations

Watch active demand and intervene when a rider is unassigned or a service state breaks.

PENPOT MOBILE PROTOTYPE

Five screens. One connected pickup flow.

These are browser counterparts of the real Penpot boards—not invented dashboard cards.

DESIGN DECISIONS

Built around the next useful action.

  1. Maps provide context.They show the moving vehicle and route without replacing essential trip controls.
  2. Identity is visible at pickup.Driver name, vehicle and plate sit next to actions a rider may need immediately.
  3. Driver flow stays operational.Turn instruction, ETA, pickup location, support and OTP are kept in one navigation state.
  4. Safety is not hidden.It remains a named action when a rider is waiting or travelling.
